# Technical-regime transfer

## Regime definitions

| Era | Role | Direct physics evidence |
|---|---|---:|
| 2019 pre-Aeroscreen | Supporting backward comparison | 10 transitions |
| 2020–2024 Aeroscreen, pre-hybrid | Frozen reference core | 41 transitions |
| 2025 Aeroscreen + hybrid | External regime | 23 direct physics transitions; 15 external inference cases |
| 2026 qualifying format | Applicability boundary | No same-day initial-round repeats by structure |

## Coefficient comparison

The robust point estimates retain the same coefficient directions across 2019, 2020–2024 and 2025. All reported 90% bootstrap intervals for direct coefficient differences include zero. This is no clear evidence of a regime shift within recoverable data; it is not equivalence proof.

The 2019 evidence is especially weak because `n=10` and the correlation between track- and ambient-temperature changes is 0.926. Its coefficients are not precise independent estimates. The 2025 predictor correlation is lower at 0.615 but the sample remains limited.

## Separate applicability questions

Technical transfer asks whether physical response remains sufficiently compatible. Format applicability asks whether a meaningful repeat-attempt decision exists. A system may transfer physically while becoming operationally irrelevant under a different qualifying structure.
