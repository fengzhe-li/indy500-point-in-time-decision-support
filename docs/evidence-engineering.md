# Evidence engineering

## Source authority

Official INDYCAR results and detailed reports form the attempt-performance backbone. Timing/replay material supports chronology where available but is not assumed to be permanently accessible. PTSC-hosted Firestone-named workbook data provides structured observed track/weather evidence and remains distinct from INDYCAR-official editorial anchors. HRRR is forecast/context evidence, not observed track temperature.

## Evidence classes

1. **Primary modelling:** complete official four-lap attempts with usable same-car and environmental linkage.
2. **Secondary mechanism:** section/trap measurements from complete attempts.
3. **Reconstruction only:** partial laps, incomplete or waved-off traces.
4. **Quarantined:** unresolved identity, chronology or environment linkage.

## Canonical products

- 41 frozen same-car performance transitions support the physical response.
- 168 structured 2020–2024 track-temperature observations support physical-state reconstruction.
- 656 matched current–future state pairs support the future track-temperature model.
- 39 of 41 transitions support section-level mechanism diagnostics.
- 15 eligible 2025 cases support external-regime retrospective evaluation.

These counts belong to different interfaces and must not be combined as though they were one training table.

## Provenance and quarantine

Canonical tables retain source identity, join method, coverage and eligibility. Missing values remain missing. A generic historical status does not imply lane eligibility. Attempt-to-attempt elapsed time is not queue wait. Exact decision-time state never uses later leaderboard, weather or attempt outcomes.

## Redistribution and durability

The repository includes derived tables and many raw public artefacts, but external endpoints, archive replays and source licensing can limit complete raw-data replay. Freeze manifests and hashes preserve the exact analytical state even when an external source later changes.
