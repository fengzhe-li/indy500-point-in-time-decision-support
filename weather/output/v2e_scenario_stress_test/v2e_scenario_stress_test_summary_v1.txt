INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This stress test evaluates the behavior of the frozen conditional physical-performance system.
It does NOT estimate queue waiting time, opportunity probability, or retain/withdraw expected utility.
A scenario with higher P(improvement) therefore means that, conditional on receiving another on-track opportunity at that horizon and physical state, improvement is more probable under the model.
It must NOT be interpreted as a recommendation to withdraw an existing qualifying result.
