## Frozen pipeline consequences

- Lane, withdrawal, requeue, and queue facts are populated only from direct or specifically cited editorial evidence. Missing facts remain `UNKNOWN`.
- Timing71 filename/recorder time remains `RECORDER_CAPTURE` and maps to `APPROXIMATE_OBSERVED` event timing at best.
- Section segmentation for 2020-2023 is a versioned reconstruction. Its outputs are enabled only after empirical section-sum validation and result-tuple matching.
- The 2024 Section format is validated separately and supplies selected-attempt sections only.
- HRRR index files establish archive inventory, not forecast values or decision-time availability. Unknown availability prevents state/forecast joins.
